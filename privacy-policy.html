Privacy Policy & Architecture

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Our Core Privacy Guarantee

OnePageTools is engineered with a privacy-first, client-side execution model. When you convert, compress, merge, decrypt, or manipulate files, the computation executes directly inside your browser memory using JavaScript and WebAssembly. Your documents never leave your device and are never transmitted to any external backend server.

1. How In-Browser File Processing Works

Traditional file converter websites require you to upload private files to a remote server, where they sit in cloud storage queues before being processed and sent back.

In contrast, OnePageTools uses modern browser capabilities:

  • PDF Processing: Handled by pdf-lib and Mozilla's PDF.js directly in your browser thread.
  • Image Processing: Handled via HTML5 Canvas API hardware acceleration.
  • Spreadsheet & Data: Parsed client-side using SheetJS (xlsx) and standard DOM APIs.
  • ZIP Packaging: Built in-memory via JSZip.

2. What Data We Collect

Because we do not require account registration or login, we collect:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): No names, email addresses, or phone numbers are requested or stored to use the tools.
  • No File Data or Metadata: File contents, document titles, and images stay exclusively in your browser session.
  • Standard Anonymous Web Analytics: Standard aggregated metrics (such as page views, browser type, and general geographic country) to monitor system health and performance.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser localStorage solely to remember your UI preferences, such as your dark/light theme choice (site-theme). No tracking cookies are used to profile users across other websites.
